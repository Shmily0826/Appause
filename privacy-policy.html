Appause Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-07-29 · Updated: 2026-10-23

Appause is built around a single promise: your groups, cooldowns, and usage stats never leave your device. The only exceptions are things you start yourself — Pro activation and feedback — and this policy spells out exactly what they send.

This document explains what we collect, what we don't, and the permissions the app uses. It is written for non-experts. If anything is unclear, open an issue on the project repository.

What we collect

Almost nothing. Appause does not collect or transmit your personal information. Your app groups, cooldowns, and usage stats are stored locally and never leave your device.

  • There is no account, no login, and no registration.
  • The app makes no network requests during normal use — no analytics, no crash reporters, no advertisements, and no third-party SDKs.
  • The network is used in three situations, all of them things you start: starting the Pro trial, redeeming a code, and sending feedback. Starting a trial or redeeming a code sends a device fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash of an on-device key) to the activation server; the server returns a signed license token that is verified on your device and works offline afterwards. The server keeps that fingerprint together with the trial or redemption time, the expiry time, and the license status, so each device gets one trial and each code binds to at most 3 devices. The fingerprint is generated on your device and contains no name, phone number, or hardware serial, but it stays the same until Appause is uninstalled, so it is a pseudonymous device identifier. The server never receives your groups, stats, or which apps you use. It holds no browsing history, messages, or app usage.
  • Feedback is sent only when you choose to (Settings → Feedback); the app never uploads it automatically. Before sending, the app shows your message, optional contact detail, device/app metadata, and a diagnostic snapshot. The snapshot may include service and permission state, recent foreground/interception state, configured group names, and package names. It does not include screen content, messages, account information, or a chronological usage history. You can send the displayed report through Appause, email, or a pre-filled GitHub issue.
  • All configuration (your app groups, cooldowns, and usage stats) is stored locally in an on-device database (Room) and preferences store (DataStore).

How the Accessibility Service is used

Appause uses Android's AccessibilityService only to detect which app is currently in the foreground, by reading its package name.

  • canRetrieveWindowContent is set to false. We do not read your screen content, do not take screenshots, and do not record anything you type or see.
  • We only use the package name to decide whether to show the pause screen for a group you configured.
  • Appause excludes itself, the system launcher, and system UI from interception.

This is an accessibility / habit-forming feature, not a monitoring or security tool.

Your control over data

  • Your groups, cooldowns, and stats live on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them permanently.
  • If you started a trial or redeemed a code, the pseudonymous license record described above stays on the activation server; uninstalling does not remove it.
  • You can review or delete any group at any time from within the app.

Children

Appause is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Apart from the pseudonymous license record created when you start a trial or redeem a code, and feedback you choose to send, it collects no data.
