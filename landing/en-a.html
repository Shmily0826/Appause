A speed bump, not a wall

Appause never locks you out. It makes continuing an intentional choice.

⏳ Cooldown pause

A full-screen pause with a live countdown covers the target app the instant it opens. Watch the seconds and decide.

🏷️ Open with a reason

Continuing requires picking a reason — work, bored, messages. Your log becomes a mirror.

🚫 Cancel escapes

Cancel sends you straight back to the launcher. The impulse is interrupted, not indulged.

⏱️ Temporary pass

Really need it right now? Grant a 5/15/30-minute pass. It expires by itself.

🌱 Alternatives

The pause screen suggests apps you chose as better defaults — the swap happens before the feed loads.

📊 Visible progress

Today's completed/avoided counts plus 7-day and 365-day trends. What gets measured gets managed.

Trust, by architecture

Not a privacy policy you have to believe — a design you can verify.

Reads package names only

The accessibility service checks which app is foreground and nothing else. Window-content retrieval is explicitly disabled in its configuration.

Local-first storage

Every record lives in the app's own database. There is no server that could receive it, and no SDK that could copy it.

Auditable

The full source is on GitHub, including exactly how the accessibility permission is used. You don't have to trust us — read it.

Honest disclosure: Appause needs Android's Accessibility permission to detect app openings. This is an accessibility use, not surveillance — and the permission dialog says so. Uninstall and every trace is gone.
